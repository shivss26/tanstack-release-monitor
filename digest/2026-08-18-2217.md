## Changes

- Fixed a regression where switching tabs or params on an already-loaded page briefly replaced the whole view with a full-page loading state instead of keeping content visible while data reloads.
- Reworked how failed or interrupted navigations are handled internally, removing a broad safety-net that used to catch all failures at the top level; errors now surface on the specific route where they occurred instead of being silently rolled back, so some previously-hidden failures may now become visible (breaking change).
- Reduced bundle size by exporting the error-catching boundary component more directly, with no change in behavior for most apps.
